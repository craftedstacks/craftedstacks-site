Measured · Help and support

Support

Measured is a financial-calculator toolkit from Crafted Stacks. No account is currently required.

Email support

Saved calculations

Use Save on a calculator result and optionally group it into a project. Open Saved from the home screen to restore, organize, or remove calculations.

Unexpected results

Confirm every input and review the assumptions shown below the calculator. Measured provides educational estimates; lender, provider, tax, investment, and government-program rules may differ.

Reporting a problem

Include the calculator name, the screen where the problem occurred, your iOS version, and a description of what you expected. Do not email account credentials, Social Security numbers, full bank information, or other sensitive documents.

Data requests

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to support@craftedstacks.net.
