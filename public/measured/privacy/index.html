Privacy Policy

Effective August 19, 2026

Measured is a calculator app that works without an account. This policy explains how saved work and limited product analytics are handled.

Your saved work stays on your device. We use limited analytics to improve the calculators and app reliability. We do not sell your saved calculations.

Using Measured

Calculations run in the app. Projects and calculations are stored only when you choose to save them, using private app or browser storage. On iOS, saved app data may be included in an enabled device or iCloud backup. A backup is not live synchronization.

Product analytics

We use limited product analytics to understand which calculators and features are useful, improve calculator behavior, diagnose failures, and protect the service. Analytics can include:

  • the calculator and category used;
  • numeric or option values entered and calculated results;
  • feature actions, such as opening, saving, or removing an item;
  • random session and calculation identifiers;
  • app version, platform, approximate region derived by our analytics provider, and sanitized error categories.

Analytics does not include save or project names, saved-record or project IDs, search text, contact information, bank credentials, advertising IDs, persistent device identifiers, full URLs, complete local-storage contents, or raw error messages.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to operate, secure, analyze, and improve Measured. Microsoft Azure Application Insights processes product analytics and diagnostics for us. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, during a business transaction, or when you direct us to share it.

Measured does not currently use targeted advertising and does not sell calculator inputs, saved calculations, or synced financial content.

Storage, retention, and choices

Local saved content remains until you remove it, clear app or browser data, or uninstall the app. Backup copies follow your Apple backup settings. Anonymous product analytics is generally retained for no more than 90 days. Because analytics is not linked to an account or persistent device identifier, we may not be able to locate an individual analytics event.

You can use the calculators without an account, choose whether to save a calculation, remove saved items in the app, and manage device backups in Apple settings.

Future features

Measured may later offer optional accounts, cloud sync, purchases, recommendations, or support features. Those services may require additional information to work. We will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required before using information for a materially different purpose.

Security and children

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle, although no transmission or storage method is completely secure. Measured is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Measured changes. The current version and effective date will remain available here. Questions and privacy requests can be submitted through the Measured Support page.
