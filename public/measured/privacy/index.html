Measured

Privacy and Data Policy

Issued by Crafted Stacks | Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Introduction and general terms

This policy applies to Measured, a financial calculator application developed by Crafted Stacks ("Measured," "we," "us," or "our"). It explains how information is handled when you use the app and related website.

Measured works without an account. You can choose to save projects and calculations for later.

2. Information we may process

Measured uses limited product analytics. This may include the calculator and category used, values entered, calculated results, app actions, random session and calculation identifiers, app version, platform, approximate region, and a general error category.

Analytics does not include save or project names, saved-record or project IDs, search text, contact information, bank credentials, advertising IDs, persistent device identifiers, full URLs, complete local-storage contents, or raw error messages.

If you contact us for support, we may process the contact details and information you choose to provide in order to respond.

3. Why we process information

We use product analytics to understand which tools are useful, improve calculator behavior and recommendations, diagnose problems, protect the service, and maintain a reliable experience. We use support information only to respond to requests, investigate reported problems, and improve Measured.

4. Saved calculations and backups

Measured currently uses app or browser storage for saved projects and calculations. On iOS, this data may be included in an enabled device or iCloud backup. Storage options may evolve as we add features such as accounts or synchronization; we will explain material changes and provide applicable choices.

5. Service providers and disclosure

Microsoft Azure Application Insights processes product analytics and diagnostics on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law, when reasonably necessary to protect users or the service, as part of a business transaction, or when you ask us to share it.

Measured does not currently use targeted advertising and does not sell calculator inputs or saved calculations.

6. Retention, choices, and rights

Anonymous product analytics is generally retained for no more than 90 days. Because it is not linked to an account or persistent device identifier, we may not be able to locate an individual analytics event.

You can use Measured without an account, decide whether to save a calculation, remove saved items in the app, and manage device backups in Apple settings. Depending on where you live, applicable law may also provide rights relating to personal information we hold about you.

7. Security and children

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we process, although no transmission or storage method is completely secure. Measured is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

Measured may later offer optional accounts, cloud sync, purchases, recommendations, or support features. We will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required before introducing a materially different use of information. The current policy and effective date will remain available on this page.

9. Contact information

Questions, comments, and privacy requests can be submitted through the Measured Support page.
