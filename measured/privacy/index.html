Privacy Policy

Effective August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Measured ("Measured," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application, website, calculators, saved projects, and any optional account, cloud, paid, recommendation, or support features we offer (collectively, the "Services").

Privacy at a glance

  • Calculations run on your device. Numeric inputs, selected options, and generated results may also be included in limited product analytics.
  • Saved calculations remain in private app or browser storage. Saved names, project names, and saved-record IDs are not sent with telemetry.
  • No account is currently required. Future accounts, sync, and paid services will be optional unless a feature clearly states otherwise.
  • Measured may be monetized through purchases, paid plans, or compensated recommendations. We do not sell calculator inputs, saved calculations, or synced financial content.

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers information handled by Measured through the Services. The Services may evolve to include optional accounts, cloud synchronization, paid plans, one-time purchases, personalized recommendations, affiliate offers, sponsored content, notifications, and customer support. Describing a feature in this policy does not mean that the feature is currently available or that its related information is collected today.

This policy does not cover third-party websites, financial providers, app stores, or services that we do not control. If we link to or recommend a third-party service, that service's own privacy policy applies to information you provide to it.

2. Information we handle

Calculator and financial scenario data

Numeric values and options you enter, such as loan balances, rates, income, expenses, contributions, and terms, plus calculated results and result details. Calculations run locally; a compact copy may be sent in product analytics to evaluate calculator use and improve results and recommendations.

Saved content

Calculation names, project names, saved inputs, saved results, local project IDs, and local saved-record IDs when you choose Save. This content remains in private app or browser storage and is not included in telemetry.

Product interaction data

App launch, category and calculator opened, save or removal actions, project counts, theme changes, search-query length and result count, and the calculator selected from search. We do not collect the search text.

Session and calculation identifiers

Random session and calculation identifiers used to connect related events during an app session or calculator visit. These are not advertising identifiers or persistent cross-session device identifiers.

Device and diagnostics data

App version, build number, platform, and sanitized error type and source. We do not intentionally send raw error messages, full URLs, or complete local-storage contents.

Approximate location

Microsoft Azure may temporarily process the network IP address used to deliver telemetry and derive approximate city, state or province, and country or region. Under Azure's default masking, the IP address is then discarded and is not stored as part of Measured's telemetry.

Account and profile data

If you choose an account feature, we may collect a name or display name, email address, authentication identifier, account preferences, account status, and security activity to create, maintain, personalize, and protect the account.

Cloud and connected content

If you enable cloud sync or another connected feature, we may process the calculations, projects, preferences, or other content you choose to sync. A future financial-account connection would require a separate explanation and authorization before bank credentials or account data are accessed.

Purchase and entitlement data

If you buy a paid plan, subscription, or one-time feature, Apple or another payment processor may provide product, purchase, subscription, entitlement, renewal, refund, limited billing, and transaction-status information. Payment processors generally handle full payment-card details directly.

Recommendation and attribution data

If Measured offers personalized, sponsored, or affiliate recommendations, we may process calculator category, general scenario attributes, approximate location, offer views, clicks, referral source, and conversion or commission status to select, deliver, measure, and improve those recommendations.

Communications and support

If you contact us, join a mailing list, enable notifications, or answer a survey, we may collect your contact details, message, preferences, attachments, and related service history.

Information we do not currently collect

Measured does not currently require or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, bank credentials, connected financial-account data, credit reports, Social Security numbers, precise GPS location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or persistent cross-session device identifiers. We do not include project names, calculation names, saved-record IDs, project IDs, or search text in telemetry. Some of these categories may be collected only if you later choose a feature described above.

Sources of information

Information may come from values and choices you enter in the Services, actions you take in the app, device or network context, Apple or another payment processor, providers you authorize, support and recommendation partners, and other users you choose to collaborate with. We do not currently obtain personal or financial information about you from data brokers, credit bureaus, banks, or account-aggregation providers.

  • process purchases, subscriptions, entitlements, renewals, and refunds;

  • evaluate calculator behavior and improve accuracy, design, and recommendations;
  • diagnose errors, protect the Services, prevent misuse, and secure accounts;
  • send service messages and, where permitted, marketing or promotional communications; and
  • comply with law and enforce our terms.

We may create aggregated or de-identified statistics that do not reasonably identify an individual and use them to operate, analyze, and improve Measured.

6. Local storage, cloud services, and backups

Projects and calculations are currently stored only when you choose Save. The iOS app stores them in private app preferences; the web version uses browser storage. On iOS, saved app data may be included in your iPhone or iCloud backup when backup is enabled in Apple settings. Restoring that backup may restore saved calculations.

Future account or sync features may store chosen content with a cloud provider so it can be accessed across devices, restored, shared, or used to provide personalized services. The feature will identify what is uploaded and provide applicable controls.

Current backup is not synchronization. Measured does not currently provide live cross-device sync. Clearing browser or app data, or uninstalling without an available backup, may permanently remove saved items.

7. Retention

Local saved content remains until you remove it, clear the app or browser data, or uninstall the app. Backup copies are retained according to your Apple backup settings and Apple's retention practices.

Future account and cloud content may be retained while the account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to permit recovery, complete deletion, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. Purchase, tax, fraud-prevention, and transaction records may be retained as required by law or legitimate business needs. Support and marketing records are retained as needed for those purposes and your preferences.

Telemetry is generally retained in Azure for no more than 90 days, unless a shorter period is configured or a longer period is reasonably required to investigate security, prevent abuse, comply with law, or resolve a dispute. Aggregated or de-identified statistics may be retained longer when they cannot reasonably identify an individual.

8. Your choices and deletion

  • You can use current calculators without creating an account.
  • You choose whether to save a calculation or enable a future cloud feature.
  • You can remove saved calculations and project groupings from Saved. Removing a project leaves its calculations in Unfiled unless you remove those calculations separately.
  • You can clear Measured's app or browser data and control iCloud backups through device or browser settings.
  • Future account holders will be able to manage account information, synced content, purchases, communications, and applicable recommendation or advertising choices.
  • You can unsubscribe from promotional email and disable push notifications using the message or device controls provided.

Because current telemetry is not linked to an account or persistent device identifier, we may not be able to locate a particular anonymous telemetry event in response to a request.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information we handle, including limiting telemetry fields, avoiding persistent tracking identifiers, and relying on platform and service-provider security controls. Future account and cloud features may add authentication, access controls, encryption, monitoring, and recovery safeguards appropriate to those services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
